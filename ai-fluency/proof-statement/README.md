# What Are You Proving?

This package completes the Week 1 General AI Fluency assignment **What Are You
Proving?**. It contains the final proof statement, the one-line reason the portfolio
needs to exist, and a concise record of the AI-assisted interview used to narrow the
claim without outsourcing authorship.

## Deliverables

- [`proof-statement.md`](proof-statement.md) — the final one-paragraph statement and
  one-line why.
- [`ai-interview.md`](ai-interview.md) — the questions, evidence-based answers,
  challenge, and revision decisions.

Every numerical claim traces to the verified ML capstone report. The statement does
not claim production deployment, causal impact, independent third-party review, or
consistent performance for every client.
