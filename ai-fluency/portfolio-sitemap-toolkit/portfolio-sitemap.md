# Portfolio sitemap

## The smallest useful path

```text
HOME
  |-- primary CTA: View the work --------------------.
  |                                                   v
  '-- secondary CTA: Contact Hisham              WORK / CASE STUDIES
                                                      |
                                                      |-- FlyRank capstone
                                                      |-- Reproducible ML pipeline
                                                      '-- Responsible AI workflow
                                                               |
                                                               v
ABOUT (brief credibility context) <------------- CONTACT / INVITATION
```

The global navigation stays limited to **Work**, **About**, and **Contact**. The homepage
is the landing page, so it does not need a separate navigation label.

## Why each page earns its place

| Page | Job against the proof statement | Evidence or content | Route to the one action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home | Makes the ML decision-support claim understandable in seconds. | Proof statement, one verified capstone result, and a clear human-review boundary. | Primary **View the work** CTA; secondary **Contact Hisham** CTA. |
| Work / Case studies | Proves the claim with reproducible artifacts instead of adjectives. | FlyRank opportunity-ranking capstone; end-to-end ML pipeline; responsible AI workflow. Each case shows problem, boundary, method, baseline, results, limitations, and verification. | Every case ends with a compact invitation to discuss the work. |
| About | Explains who made the work and how I operate without turning into a biography. | Short background, working principles, tools I can defend, GitHub, and LinkedIn. | Ends with the same contact invitation; no unrelated personal content. |
| Contact | Removes friction from the only requested action. | A short invitation and professional contact links. | Directly supports a role, internship, or project conversation. |

## Case-study evidence boundary

The lead case may state only verified receipts: 86,574 eligible pages; six-client grouped
holdout with zero client overlap; random-forest median client NDCG@20 of 0.797 versus a
0.690 rule baseline; precision@20 of 0.850 versus 0.917; and a six-client bootstrap interval
of 0.405–0.876. It must also state that the output is a human-reviewed prioritization queue,
not an automatic editing system or evidence of causal traffic lift.

## Things deliberately excluded

- A separate skills page: skills require evidence and belong inside case studies or About.
- A services page: I am asking for a junior role or collaboration, not selling a service menu.
- A blog at launch: it would add maintenance without strengthening the current proof.
- Testimonials: none have been verified for this portfolio.
- Multiple contact destinations: one repeated invitation is clearer than competing actions.

## Change adopted after pressure testing

The initial draft treated the ML pipeline as a second independent case study. The revised
map makes the pipeline the **method / reproducibility section inside the FlyRank capstone**
until there is a genuinely separate project with its own problem, dataset, evaluation, and
result. This avoids making one body of work look like two projects. The Work page launches
with one deep flagship case plus one smaller responsible-AI workflow case.
